# AppColoreando Support

AppColoreando support baseline for the public release.

## Help and issue reporting

Public support channel:
https://github.com/christyepez/AppColoreando/issues

When reporting a problem, include the platform (Android, iOS, Web or Windows), AppColoreando version, a short description, and reproduction steps. Do not post passwords, access tokens, private keys, payment data or other secrets.

## Account privacy requests

Authenticated end users can export their account data from:
`GET /api/me/account/export`

Authenticated end users can delete their account from:
`DELETE /api/me/account` with explicit confirmation.

Privileged editorial/administrative accounts are excluded from self-deletion and require administrator handling.

## Security reports

Do not publish credentials, tokens or exploitable secrets in a public issue. Security-sensitive reports should be handled through a private repository/security contact configured by the project owner before public store launch.

## Store publication

The repository-hosted support and privacy pages provide stable public baseline URLs. A branded support domain may replace these URLs later without changing the application data model or privacy controls.
